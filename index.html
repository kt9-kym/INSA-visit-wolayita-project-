About Marke Market

Connecting Wolaita Sodo's community through commerce

Our Story

Marke Market was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to bring Wolaita Sodo's vibrant local market online. We connect farmers, artisans, and local businesses with buyers across the region.

From fresh agricultural products to handcrafted traditional items, we celebrate the rich culture and entrepreneurial spirit of the Wolaita people.
